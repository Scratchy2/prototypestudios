what does any of this even mean?

you roll a color and it gives you badges based on the digits of the color. while you're rolling, you can get "meta badges" that can be earned multiple times. once the game finishes scoring, you get three special badges that award based on the actual color, like you'd expect. sp stands for saturation points, bp for brightness points, and ap for aesthetic points (which you can get negative chroma for). after everything finishes, you're able to share your roll by clicking it and roll again or treat the game like a daily and click the "I'm Satisfied" button to lock rolling until midnight UTC the next day.
